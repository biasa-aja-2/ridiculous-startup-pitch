One sentence, 200 characters at most, pitching a bizarre startup idea that sounds absurd until you realise it would actually sell: rent out strangers' grandmothers for ten-minute video calls that scold you into finishing your thesis. Students ignore every productivity app — but nobody ignores grandma, and accountability coaching is a market people already pay for.

> We rent out strangers' grandmothers for ten-minute video calls that scold you into finishing your thesis, because students ignore every productivity app, but never grandma.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program self-checks the kit's gates (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, one line, not whitespace-only) and refuses to write if any gate fails, then writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` and verifies it by reading it back.
